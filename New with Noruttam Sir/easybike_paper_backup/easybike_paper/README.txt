WHAT CHANGED WITH THE SURVEY (n = 43, 8-28 Oct 2026)
  Inputs now from the survey: set prices, replacement intervals, resale values, daily distance, pack size, charges per day.
  Cycle life is calibrated to the survey median interval at Khulna. NMC is removed (no NMC users in the survey).
  Main result: with survey prices and lives, LFP is dearer for the operator; break-even LFP price/life are reported.
  "Powder" batteries and charging-cost answers are not modelled (chemistry unconfirmed; mixed bases).
